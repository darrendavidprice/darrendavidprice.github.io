Particle Physics · The University of Manchester

Chasing the
invisible — at colliders, underground, and in the data.

I'm Darren Price, a particle physicist and Professor at the University of Manchester. I search for dark matter at the Large Hadron Collider and with the DarkSide experiment, as well as development of sensors for rare event detection (using silicon photosensors and optomechanical levitating sensors) including detection of high frequency gravitational waves, and have long-standing research interests across electroweak physics, QCD, B-physics and neutrino physics, and build AI tools for scientific discovery — working across several research communities at once. I also serve as Associate Dean for Public, Civic, and Schools Engagement in the Faculty of Science & Engineering.
