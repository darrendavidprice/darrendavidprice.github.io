Quantum chromodynamics governs the strong force. Even when it is not the new physics we are chasing, QCD shapes almost every signature at a hadron collider — so getting it right is essential to everything else.

Key areas

My QCD work spans vector-boson production in association with heavy-flavour jets, double parton scattering — two separate hard interactions within a single collision — and jet physics. A comprehensive Tevatron study of W+jets production introduced novel observables that exposed shortcomings in the best predictions of the day and improved the modelling used across later LHC measurements and searches.

Active now

I contribute to the roadmap for quarkonium physics at the High-Luminosity LHC, where quarkonia serve as precision probes of QCD and of proton structure.
